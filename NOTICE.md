# Attribution and validation data

pyTRACMT is a Python reimplementation designed to reproduce the principal workflow and numerical behavior of TRACMT. The associated methods should be cited using the publications listed in `README.md`.

The `sample_Usui` validation dataset is provided by Yoshiya Usui and is included/distributed with his permission for validation and reproducibility of pyTRACMT. Yoshiya Usui is a co-author of the associated SoftwareX manuscript.
